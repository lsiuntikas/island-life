Octagon

A sandstone labyrinth 476 metres across, built from your ArchiCAD model. Its walls are 3.2 metres tall, so you can’t see over them.

Every time you enter, you start somewhere different. Somewhere on the outer wall there is one way out. Time here runs on your own clock, so if it’s night where you are, bring the lantern.
