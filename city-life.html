City LifeShibuya, 1 × 1 km

Start at the scramble crossing and explore a whole square kilometre of streets, shops, parks and a shrine. Run deliveries for yen, rent a bike, eat at konbini and get home before you’re out of energy.

The phone’s World tab rebuilds any 100 m to 1 km of the real world from OpenStreetMap, with real heights, roof shapes, colours and building parts wherever they’re mapped.

WASD or arrow keys to walk, Shift to run, drag to look around, scroll to zoom, E to interact, P for the phone, M for the map, B to ride a bike, V for first person, G to change the pixel-art look.
